# D-251 — May transit-network depend on a library that holds no kind?

Topic: The libraries.
State: Unanswered.
Context: the arch gate holds transit-network to one dependency,
transit-version (`crates/cap-abi/src/bin/common/gates.rs`; its message
says a library that joins a document to the network depends on the
network, never the reverse). Two shared helpers need a home that
transit-network can use:
- The distance (D-250) lives in transit-geo, which depends on no
  crate. transit-network keeps its own copy of the same formula
  (`listing.rs`, `haversine_m`), so its answers agree, but the formula
  is written twice.
- The service date. About 22 places parse "YYYY-MM-DD" and about 25
  write it, with chrono, and they disagree on what they accept: the
  network's serde helper (`model.rs`) trims spaces, transit-notes
  requires exactly 10 characters, and the rest do neither. Ten crates
  keep their own `parse_date`. Every caller depends on
  transit-version, and transit-network and transit-sync each keep a
  serde helper for the date. GTFS's "YYYYMMDD" stays in transit-codec
  and transit-rt: it is the boundary's format, not the wire's.
Options:
- The gate lets transit-network depend on transit-version and on any
  library with no workspace dependency (recommended). A library with
  no workspace dependency cannot hold a kind (a kind needs
  transit-version's tag), so the gate's reason still holds. transit-geo
  qualifies now. A new leaf, transit-date, holds the service date: its
  parse, its format, and its serde helper. Cost: a gate rule and its
  fixture test, and one more small crate.
- Both helpers go into transit-version, which every library already
  uses. No gate change. Cost: transit-version's topic is what a
  document says about itself; it grows to hold geometry and dates, and
  takes chrono as a dependency.
- transit-network keeps its own distance and date code, and a law
  test holds its distance equal to transit-geo's. The shared date
  helper serves every other crate. Cost: the duplication this work
  removes stays in the core model.
