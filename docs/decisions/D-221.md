# D-221 — Does a station's stop_id in an alert reach its bays?

Topic: Live operations.
State: Encoded provisionally.
Context: GTFS-rt's EntitySelector says only "The stop_id from the GTFS
feed that this selector refers to". Neither gtfs.org page says whether
a parent station covers its platforms. On C-TRAN every trip leaves from
a bay (Fisher's Landing Transit Center has 9), and the search offers
the station beside its bays.
Options (encoded, with the other answer):
- **The stop_id is matched as written.** A station's id selects no
  departure at its bays; the station's pooled board shows it. The alert
  form's "What riders see" says so beside the entity, and asks for the
  bays. The other answer was to widen a station to its bays, which
  publishes a feed that other apps may read differently.
