# router

Trip planning (RAPTOR) — how do I get from here to there — over the NETWORK document and the effective day (the model's materialized trips, patched with the edits in force — the same transit-dispatch composition every day view starts from), at the live document's predicted times when one is named. Its facets: leaving at a time (earliest arrival), arriving by a time (the latest departure; with no deadline, the last bus home), and the diversion delta (the unpatched day, one materialization apart, with the rider's notice). Assemble for users who need directions, not just departure times. Documents are hash-addressed; writes nothing.

## plan

Earliest-arrival itinerary from `from` to `to` at a departure time over the materialized day. Each end is a stop id (a bare string) or a PLACE off the network — {id, name, walks: [{stop_id, walk_secs}]}, its walks to the stops near it (the streets capability's access answers them; a place id naming a stop is refused): the trip walks from the place to one of its stops, rides, and walks from one of its stops to the place, or walks straight there when `direct` ({walk_secs, path}: the walk between the two ends, when one joins them — the streets capability's access answers it) arrives as early; the itinerary and its first and last walk legs name the place by its id and name. The day: edits applied when edits_hash names a registered document; each trip instance at its PREDICTED times where the live document named by live_hash states them, schedule elsewhere, a trip it cancels never ridden and a stop it skips never boarded or alighted at; transfers along the streets when walks_hash names a registered walk table, straight lines otherwise. A null itinerary = unreachable within max_legs. The legs speak model vocabulary: the trip instance is addressed (trip, start_secs); a transit leg carries the route's short label (route_name) and long name (route_long_name, null when it has none), the headsign the bus shows at the boarding visit (the visit's own, else the trip's, else the final stop's place), and board_delay_secs / alight_delay_secs — how far a predicted time moved from the day's own (null = no prediction, the day's time). Door to door: leave_secs is when the rider leaves the origin (the first ride's boarding minus the walk before it; with no ride, depart_secs), duration_secs is arrival_secs minus leave_secs, and leave is the board's wait rule from depart_secs to leave_secs (due under a minute, whole minutes under an hour, else the clock time) — "leave in 7 min".

## plan_arrive_by

Arrive-by: the LATEST departure from `from` that reaches `to` by arrive_by_secs within max_legs, over the same day and the same ends as plan (edits_hash, live_hash, walks_hash; stops or places, direct). With no arrive_by_secs, the latest departure that reaches `to` at all on the service day — the last bus home (times past 86400 are after-midnight trips of the same day). The answer carries the deadline it answered (arrive_by_secs: the request's, else the day's last arrival) and the itinerary — the earliest-arrival plan leaving at that latest departure (its depart_secs; leave_secs, duration_secs and leave as in plan) — or null when nothing arrives by the deadline.

## plan_compare

The diversion delta: the same plan (its ends stops or places, as plan's) over the patched day (active) and the unpatched materialization (baseline, null when no edit is in force on the date or no edits_hash is given), both at the live document's predictions when live_hash is given and walking the walk table named by walks_hash, with the rider's notice whenever the diversion changes the trip — {kind: unreachable (no active trip; the baseline has one) | no_trip (none within max_legs in either world) | later (the active trip arrives later: delay_secs, minutes_lost rounded up) | diverted (it rides a trip whose patched stop times differ from its unpatched instance), baseline_arrival_secs, routes and skipped_stops of the diverted trips the plans ride, text} — null when nothing changed. One op whether or not edits exist.

## itinerary_paths

Where an itinerary runs, one path per leg ({kind: transit | walk, path: [{lat, lon}]}) over the same effective day it was planned on (pass the same edits_hash): a transit leg's RIDDEN segment — its trip's line (trip_path's: the authored alignment, an in-force diversion's traced detour spliced between its kept stops) sliced between the board and alight stops when the trip visits both, its stops between them otherwise, a straight line as the last resort — and a walk through the stops it passes — and, given the plan's ends (from, to) and direct, a walk from or to a place along that place's walk path (between two places, direct's path); without them a place draws nothing. Send the itinerary plan answered. The map draws the paths; it computes none.
