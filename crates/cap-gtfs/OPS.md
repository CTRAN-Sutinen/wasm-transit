# gtfs

The FEED document: the feed's own question over a GTFS static zip the kernel registered by the content hash of its bytes (the kernel's load_feed answers what it contains at a glance — its summary): its identity (feed_info.txt: version, publisher). The day views and the directory questions (pickers, names join) are NOT here: they answer over the NETWORK document (the board, runcut, calendar, and directory capabilities — one question, one answer on the bus). It has no ops that author anything.

## feed_info

The feed's own metadata (version, publisher), when the feed provides feed_info.txt — the identity question: which feed is this?
